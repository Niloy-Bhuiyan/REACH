Job Detail decisions
One anatomy, three verdict skins. Header, verdict block, hard requirements, preferred + missing, eligibility, provenance rail. Only the verdict colour, the sentence and the primary action change.
The verdict is a sentence, not a score. “You meet every hard requirement” / “One requirement stands between you and this role: advanced SQL.” No percentages anywhere on this screen.
Every requirement row carries its evidence and the source it came from — CV, GitHub, Portfolio — so the verdict is auditable line by line.
Provenance is a rail, not a footnote: source, first seen, last verified, posting age, deadline, plus the dedupe note explaining why one listing appears once.
Missing items are separated by consequence. A missing hard requirement drives the verdict; a missing preferred item is shown as an optional gain with its cross-role impact.
Eligibility is explicit — work authorisation, graduation window, location/mode, experience floor — because these are the only things that produce a true Skip.
Desktop keeps context: a 452px Discovery list stays visible at 68% opacity with the open row selected, and Previous / Next walk the group without returning to the list.
Mobile is a sheet over the dimmed results, ordered verdict → why you fit → gap → actions, with a sticky bar carrying Save, a secondary, and the one verdict-coloured primary at 50px.
Art density here is the DNA’s functional band: one 40–50px verdict object per screen plus 22–26px section glyphs. Company marks are lettered tiles until real logos exist; requirement met/missing glyph pairs, a provenance mark and a deadline glyph remain on the missing-asset list.
