One Fix Away decisions
The blocker is the headline. A 52px Baloo sentence — “Say SQL out loud.” — with the gap-type badge above it. The user knows what to do before scrolling.
All three gap types are shown, not just yours. Knowledge / Proof / Signal appear as a row with your one highlighted in apricot and the others explicitly ruled out — the classification is legible, not asserted.
Gap type sets the intervention. Signal gaps get a CV-and-README fix in hours; the copy says outright that no course is needed, and the certificate verdict lives on Roadmap.
Each step names its artefact. 2 public repos, 1 portfolio entry, 2 CV lines, with hours in mono — effort and output are both visible.
The Delta is audited on the page. 54 verified → 12 already Apply Now → 8 blocked by SQL alone → 4 excluded because they need a second fix. The exclusion is stated, which is what makes the number trustworthy.
Affected jobs are a real table with other-requirement status, source, verified age and the verdict each row becomes after the fix — every one clickable back into Job Detail.
Evidence for the classification is shown — the two repos with SQL inside, the private one, the CV with no mention — so “signal gap” is a conclusion the user can check.
Mobile splits the job in two. Screen one is the decision (gap type, delta, three steps, roadmap); screen two is the audit sheet listing the 8. Sticky bar carries the CV shortcut and the roadmap primary.
Narrative density: one 96px wrench object, a 50%-opacity scenery bookend behind the header, and 22–30px section glyphs — above Discovery, below Home, per DNA v2. The three gap-type emblems and a “doors opening” delta motif are still on the missing-asset list; letters K / P / S stand in per the fallback ladder. Roadmap itself is untouched.
