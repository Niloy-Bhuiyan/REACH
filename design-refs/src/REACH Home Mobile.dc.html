Recomposition decisions
Hero at 178px, display 34px. One scenery band, masked top and bottom so the navy headline keeps contrast. Target pill stays under the copy exactly as on desktop. Three status cards → a 3-up count strip. Counts are the fastest possible answer to “what do I have today”, and each tile carries colour + number + word with a 3px verdict rule. Approved priority order. Target hero → verdict strip → Biggest Blocker → Fastest Fix with the Delta → Apply Now preview → One Fix Away preview → Skip (collapsed) → Roadmap → Connect your world → profile completion → bottom nav. Real opportunities sit above the plan: Roadmap follows the previews rather than preceding them. Opportunity Delta moved into Fastest Fix as its one-row mobile form, so the 12 → 20 evidence travels with the fix rather than needing its own card. Lists show 2 rows + “see all n”. Rows go two-line: title and freshness on line one, company · city · mode on line two, then the evidence chip and the gap type. Skip collapses to a single review row directly under the two previews, closing the triage group. Nothing is hidden — the count and the three blockers stay visible — but it never outranks what you can act on. Connect your world keeps both groups. Evidence sources become full-width rows with status; engines stay a 4-up chip grid with the configuration pointer to Profile → Settings. Bottom nav = the same five destinations at 56px targets with pixel glyphs, plus the home-indicator inset. No hamburger, no nested drawers.
3B and 3C reuse 3A's components unchanged — only the header treatment and the populated data differ. Bottom-nav glyphs are placeholders drawn from the existing motif library per the DNA fallback ladder — the five dedicated 28–34px nav glyphs are still on the missing-asset list, as is a 390-wide scenery crop authored at that width.
