Resume Builder decisions
Two meters, permanently separate. ATS Readiness carries “no job involved” in its own footer; JD Match names the posting and says “recomputed per job, never averaged”. Neither shows a 0–100 number — both lead with a word. Analysis is a tab over the editor, not a third column. Editor 480px, analysis fluid, ivory preview 494px — three panes, but the middle one switches between Analysis / Suggested edits / Keywords rather than stacking more surfaces. Keywords are grouped by consequence: high-priority missing in terracotta, lower-priority missing in apricot, matched in sage. Colour never carries the meaning alone — each group is labelled. Every edit is before / after / why / evidence / accept / reject. The evidence row names the repo, the file count and the date, so the user can check the claim rather than trust it. Unsupported claims are visibly blocked. The fabricated-metric case ships as a terracotta card with a disabled Accept, an explanation, and a route to add real evidence — the only path to that sentence is proving it first. The editor states evidence health per section — 7 of 9 skills backed, 1 project without a link, publications empty and hidden — so gaps are visible where they are edited. Pending edits are marked in the preview with a violet rule and the word “pending”; nothing is written to the document until accepted, and accepting creates a new version rather than overwriting. Mobile is three tabs, never three panes. Analysis (both meters + priority keywords), Edits (one card at a time with the blocked state), Preview (ivory, pending marks, save). Each has one sticky primary action at 48–50px.
Art density is the DNA’s functional floor: a 24–30px CV glyph in the page header and one 22px glyph on the edits section. No scenery, nothing behind the reading surface or the tables. Still on the missing-asset list: the ATS-readiness and JD-match emblem pair, a section-reorder handle, a version glyph, matched/missing keyword marks and a “no evidence” caution mark.
