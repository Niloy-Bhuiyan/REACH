Profile decisions
Completeness is stated as capability — “enough to judge 46 of today’s 54 postings” — with the 82% as a supporting figure, not the headline. The costly gap is named at the top. No portfolio → weakens 7 One Fix Away roles, with one action. Missing evidence always says what it costs. Evidence sources and AI engines are separate sections with different visual weight: sources are a five-row settings table with contribution stated; engines are a default card plus quiet rows. Four real connection states — connected/syncing (sage dot), needs attention (apricot square + reason + Reconnect), not connected (grey dot, effect stated), missing-and-costing (violet, because it is the product’s ask). Skills carry their evidence and gap type. Proven / signal gap / proof gap / knowledge gap per row, with the role count each affects — the same vocabulary as One Fix Away. Eligibility is its own group because it is what produces Skip: authorisation, visa, graduation, experience floor, notice period — including the apricot row that blocks 6 senior roles. Inline groups, not card soup. A sticky section rail plus hairline-divided tables; the only bordered surfaces are the completeness card, the gap card, the default-engine card and the state-highlighted rows. Nothing social: no headline, no connections, no endorsements, no recruiter view, no public visibility — and a standing note that nothing here is public.
Working Mode art density: 22–34px glyphs at section heads, no scenery band, no decorative illustration. Still on the missing-asset list: a Kaggle status mark (lettered tile stands in), publication / certification / education glyphs, visa and relocation glyphs, and an engine-configuration mark.
